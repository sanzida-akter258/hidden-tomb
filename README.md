The Hidden Tomb: Game Design Document
Vision Statement
We are making a treasure-finding game, using platformer moves for the player to move and avoid obstacles and traps. We are also making the theme in the form of it being an Egyptian tomb, including monsters to attack to gain treasure and coins

Gameplay
Mechanics
The core mechanics is moving left and right, jumping across platforms, climbing ladders and attacking enemies with a basic weapon. Players make choices in when to fight, when to avoid traps and how to navigate the maps safely. Collecting coins and treasure pieces is a repeated mechanic in the game.

Scoring and/or Win / Lose Conditions
The player’s goal is to find the big room, defeat the mummy boss and win all the treasure. The player loses when its hit points reach zero

Controls
Move: Arrow Keys Jump: Spacebar Attack/Use: A Climbing is planned but is not currently implemented.

Aesthetic
The aesthetic is going for an Egyptian tomb, catacombs area. Using light browns, dark browns and gold as the main color theme. Also will be using bright colors for jewels and treasure

Desired Player Experience
The game is simple and fun, something that the player can enjoy casually. There’s not a lot of strategy, it's mostly searching and fighting.

Game Characters
PC (Treasure Hunter) name: Zack NPCs: Mummy Boss, Scarab Monster, Snakes, Bats, Scorpions

Story
The player is a treasure hunter who enters an ancient Egyptian tomb in search of treasure. As the character explores the tomb they discover traps, monsters and obstacles. Once the player makes it to the treasure room there will be a mummy boss, defeating the mummy boss allows the player to claim the treasure and escape the tomb

The Game World
The game world is an ancient Egyptian tomb located underground. It is a connected area with stone pathways, platforms, ladders, and open spaces for the player to explore. The surroundings include hieroglyphs, statues, pillars, torches, and other Egyptian decorations. The tomb has a dark and mysterious atmosphere, with gold and treasure appearing throughout the area. At the end of the tomb is a large chamber containing the main treasure.

Media List
The game will use a simple Egyptian tomb art style with mostly brown, tan, and gold colors. Coins, jewels, and treasure will use brighter colors so they stand out from the tomb environment.
